The new scrt.network is built around one question: **what can you do with SCRT today?**

So the site now starts with three clear steps: get a wallet, get SCRT, and stake it to help secure the network. Each step has its own page with the options that are actually available, explained without jargon.

## What's new

- **[SCRT](/scrt):** the coin and the network at a glance, and every way to get it: exchanges, card on-ramps, DEX swaps and bridges.
- **[Wallets](/wallets):** which wallet fits you, and how to keep it safe.
- **[Staking](/staking):** a step-by-step guide, including the 21-day unbonding period and how to choose a validator.
- **[Roadmap](/roadmap):** what has shipped and what's coming next.

## What's next

Apps built on Secret and confidential virtual machines (SecretVM) will get their own sections as they open up to everyday users. Follow the [roadmap](/roadmap) or this blog to see them land.
