Staking means locking your SCRT with a **validator**, one of the computers that produce blocks and keep Secret Network running. In return you earn a share of the network's rewards. You keep ownership of your tokens the whole time: the validator can never move them.

## What you need

- A wallet that supports Secret, such as **Keplr** or **Leap**. See [our wallet guide](/wallets).
- Some SCRT in that wallet, plus a little extra to pay transaction fees. See [how to get SCRT](/scrt#get-scrt).

## Delegate in four steps

1. Open the [Secret Dashboard](https://dash.scrt.network) and connect your wallet.
2. Go to **Staking** and browse the list of validators.
3. Pick a validator, enter the amount and confirm the transaction in your wallet.
4. Rewards start accruing right away. Claim them whenever you like, or restake them to compound.

## Choosing a validator

There is no single right answer, but a few habits help:

- **Spread your stake.** Delegating to validators outside the top few keeps the network more decentralised.
- **Check the commission.** It is the share of your rewards the validator keeps.
- **Look at uptime.** Validators that miss too many blocks get jailed, and their delegators stop earning while that lasts.

## Unstaking takes 21 days

When you unstake ("unbond"), your SCRT stays locked for **21 days** and earns nothing during that time. After that it returns to your wallet automatically. Plan around this if you might need the funds soon.

You can move stake between validators instantly with a **redelegation**, without waiting the 21 days.

## Risks to know about

If a validator misbehaves, part of the stake delegated to it can be **slashed**:

- Signing two different blocks at the same height (double signing): 5% of stake.
- Being offline for too long: 0.01% of stake, plus a temporary jail.

Choosing reliable validators keeps this risk low.

## Your vote counts too

Staked SCRT gives you a voice in governance. Each proposal is open for **7 days**. If you don't vote, your validator's vote counts for you, so it's worth checking how they vote.
